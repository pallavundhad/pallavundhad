# Selling the AI Job Hunter template

## 1. What you sell

**One product: the template.** No setup service, no hosting, no access to buyers' data.

| Included in the download | |
|---|---|
| `workflow.json` | The n8n workflow, imported in one click |
| `README.md` | Step-by-step setup guide (send it as a PDF too) |
| `applications-sheet-header.csv` | Header row for the tracking sheet |

**Price:** €29-49 one-time. Start at €29 with a launch discount, then raise it once you have reviews.

**Where to sell:** Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, or the n8n creator hub (templates section).

**The buyer brings their own AI and accounts.** They connect their own Anthropic API key, Google account and Adzuna key, and pay those services directly. So:
- you have no AI costs, however many copies you sell
- you never handle anyone's CV or credentials (much simpler GDPR)
- you stay within n8n's Sustainable Use License, which allows selling workflow templates

## 2. Listing copy

**Title:** AI Job Hunter: n8n workflow that finds jobs from your CV, tailors your CV to every job and applies

**Description:**
Stop rewriting your CV for every application. Every morning this n8n workflow:
- reads your CV and searches job boards for roles you can actually get
- saves every new job with its full job description to your tracking sheet
- gives every job a **Fit Score** and a **CV Match score** (how much of the job description your current CV covers), and lists the keywords you are missing
- rewrites your CV for each good match (only true facts, no invented experience) and saves it as a Google Doc and PDF
- writes a cover letter specific to that company, in the language of the ad
- emails the application with your tailored CV attached when the ad gives an email address
- sends you one summary email with everything ready

**What's included:** n8n workflow, step-by-step setup guide, Google Sheet template.

**You need (your own accounts):** n8n (Cloud or self-hosted), your own Anthropic API key (about $12-48 per month of AI usage depending on settings, paid to Anthropic), free Adzuna API key, Google account.

**Good to know:** jobs that require online application forms are prepared, not submitted. You get the tailored CV and the link. Results depend on your CV and market; no job or interview is guaranteed.

## 5. Before you take money (Germany)

- **Residence permit:** if you are on a student residence permit, self-employment usually needs approval from the Ausländerbehörde. Check this first.
- **Business registration:** register a Gewerbe. The Kleinunternehmerregelung (no VAT on invoices) is possible while turnover is small. Ask the Finanzamt or a Steuerberater.
- **EU VAT on digital products:** Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy and Payhip collect and pay EU VAT for you as merchant of record. That's a good reason to use them instead of selling directly.
- **Sales page:** a sales page in Germany needs an Impressum and a Datenschutzerklärung.
- **Honest marketing:** promise saved time and better-targeted applications, not jobs or interviews.
