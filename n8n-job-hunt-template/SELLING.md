# Selling the AI Job Hunter template

## 1. What you sell (three tiers)

| Tier | What the buyer gets | Suggested price |
|---|---|---|
| **Template** | `workflow.json` + `README.md` + sheet header, download on Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy / the n8n creator hub | €29-49 one-time |
| **Setup** | Template + you set it up in the buyer's own n8n and Google account on a 45-minute call, add their CV, run the first test | €149-249 one-time |
| **Managed** | Setup + monthly check of results, tuning of searches and CV prompts, adding job sources | €49-99 per month |

The buyer always runs the workflow **in their own n8n account with their own API keys and Gmail**. That keeps you within n8n's Sustainable Use License (you sell templates and consulting, not hosted n8n), and the buyer's data and costs stay in their accounts.

## 2. Product listing copy

**Title:** AI Job Hunter: n8n workflow that finds jobs from your CV, tailors your CV to every job and applies

**Short description:**
Stop rewriting your CV for every application. Every morning this n8n workflow:
- reads your CV and searches job boards for roles you can actually get
- scores every new job 0-100 for fit and saves the full job description to your tracking sheet
- rewrites your CV for each good match (only true facts, no invented experience) and saves it as a Google Doc and PDF
- writes a cover letter specific to that company
- emails the application with your tailored CV attached when the ad gives an email address
- sends you one summary email with everything ready

**What's included:** n8n workflow (import in one click), step-by-step setup guide, Google Sheet template.

**Requirements:** n8n (Cloud or self-hosted), Anthropic API key (about $12-48 per month depending on settings), free Adzuna API key, Google account.

**Note:** jobs that require online application forms are prepared, not submitted. You get the tailored CV and the link.

## 4. Before you take money (Germany)

- **Residence permit:** if you are on a student residence permit, self-employment usually needs approval from the Ausländerbehörde. Check this **before** you start selling.
- **Business registration:** register a Gewerbe at your Gewerbeamt. You can use the Kleinunternehmerregelung (no VAT on invoices) while turnover is small. Ask the Finanzamt or a Steuerberater.
- **GDPR:** for Setup / Managed you will see clients' CVs. You need a privacy policy, a simple data processing agreement (AVV), and you should not keep CVs after the setup is done.
- **Website:** any sales page in Germany needs an Impressum and a Datenschutzerklärung.
- **Honest marketing:** don't promise interviews or jobs. Promise saved time and better-targeted applications.
- **n8n license:** selling templates and setup work is fine. Hosting n8n for clients, where they log in to your instance or you run it on their credentials, needs n8n's Embed licence. Check the current license FAQ on n8n's website.
